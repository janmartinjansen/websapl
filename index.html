WebSapl

Lazy functional programming on the JMVM, compiled & executed in WebAssembly

Everything here runs entirely client-side inside your browser tab in WebAssembly: the self-hosted Sapl compiler, the Sapl+ preprocessor, the Hindley-Milner typechecker, the Clean ABC decompiler, and the JMVM virtual machine. No server or installation is required.
